What is Page RPM?

Page RPM stands for Revenue Per Mille (Mille means 1,000 in Latin). It represents the estimated earnings you generate for every 1,000 page views.

The formula is:

RPM = (Estimated Earnings / Number of Page Views) * 1000

Unlike CPC (Cost Per Click) which focuses on individual ad clicks, RPM gives you a holistic view of how well your pages monetize traffic overall. It is one of the most critical metrics for publishers using VIP Tool and Google AdSense.

Why is RPM Important?

Tracking RPM helps you identify which pages perform best. A high RPM indicates that your content is valuable to advertisers and that your ad placement is optimized. If your RPM is low, you might need to adjust your ad density, improve content quality, or target high-paying keywords.
