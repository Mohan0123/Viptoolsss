How to Use the SIP Calculator

Our AI-powered SIP Calculator is designed to help investors estimate the potential returns on their mutual fund investments. Follow these simple steps:

  • Set Monthly Investment: Use the slider to choose how much you plan to invest every month.
  • Choose Return Rate: Enter the expected annual interest rate based on the fund's past performance (typically 10-15% for equity).
  • Select Duration: Decide for how many years you intend to stay invested.

The tool instantly computes the maturity amount, showing you the power of compounding visually.
