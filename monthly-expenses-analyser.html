Take Control of Your Financial Health

Managing personal finances is the cornerstone of wealth creation. This Monthly Expenses Analyser is designed to replace complicated spreadsheets and manual calculations. By categorizing your spending, you can identify leaks in your budget and save more effectively.

How to Use This Tool

  • Enter Income: Start by inputting your total monthly take-home pay.
  • Add Expenses: Use the "Add Expense" button to list your spending. Select categories like Housing, Food, or Transport for better analysis.
  • Review Breakdown: The tool automatically calculates your total expenses and remaining balance.
  • Visual Analysis: Check the colored progress bars to see which category consumes the most of your budget.

Why Analyze Monthly Expenses?

The 50/30/20 rule suggests 50% for needs, 30% for wants, and 20% for savings. Without tracking, it is impossible to know where you stand. This tool provides an instant snapshot of your financial reality, allowing you to pivot strategies immediately.
