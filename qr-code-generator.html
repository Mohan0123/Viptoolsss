Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool free for commercial use?

Yes, VIP Tool allows you to generate unlimited QR codes for both personal and commercial projects for free.

Why isn't my QR code scanning?

Ensure there is enough contrast between the foreground and background colors. Dark foreground on light background works best.

Can I track the scans?

This tool creates "Static" QR codes which cannot be tracked. For tracking, you would need a dynamic QR code service.
